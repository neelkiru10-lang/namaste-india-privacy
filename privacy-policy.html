Privacy Policy — Namaste India

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

  • Account: mobile number, email address, name.
  • Customer trips: pickup/drop locations, date/time, vehicle choice.
  • Driver profile: vehicle details, documents (Aadhaar, Driving Licence, Vehicle RC, Insurance) for verification.Aadhaar masking: only the masked form (last 4 digits) is stored, plus a one-way hash used only to detect duplicate registrations. Your full 12-digit Aadhaar number is never stored on our servers. You give explicit consent for Aadhaar verification in the registration form before submitting. Driving licence numbers are stored the same way: masked display plus a one-way hash for duplicate detection.
  • Live location: the app does not track live location and does not send location updates to our servers. Location permission, where used, is only for actions you start yourself (for example, sharing your location from your device for SOS). Customers and drivers may also share their location directly with each other (for example over WhatsApp) if they choose to.
  • Support over WhatsApp: if you contact support via WhatsApp (for example from the login screen or the Delete-My-Data fallback), your phone number and the message you send are shared with our support team through WhatsApp.
  • Device: push notification token, app version (for reliability).

2. What we do not collect

We do not access your contacts, and we do not read the content of your SMS messages or your call history. The app does use these permissions, only for the purposes listed here:

  • Call-log / phone-state access — only for Truecaller missed-call verification (to detect the verification call). Your call history is not used for any other purpose.
  • Camera and photo gallery — only so you can take or pick your driver document photos (Aadhaar, Driving Licence, RC, Insurance).
  • SMS — only when you tap to send an SOS or contact message, the app opens your own SMS app with a pre-filled message for you to send. The app does not send SMS itself, holds no SMS permission, and does not read incoming SMS.

We do not sell your data to anyone.

3. How we use your data

  • To match customers with drivers and run the listing service.
  • To verify driver documents (admin review).
  • To send booking notifications (new booking requests, bids, booking confirmation).
  • To enforce safety rules (fraud/abuse review, account blocks for confirmed abuse).

4. Third-party services

  • Truecaller — one-tap login verification.
  • Razorpay — payments for the driver listing subscription (₹300 for 30 days) only. Trip fare is paid in cash directly to the driver; we never handle it.
  • OpenStreetMap-based routing (Valhalla / OSRM) — road-distance calculation only. No Google Maps API is used. Your pickup and drop coordinates are sent to the routing provider for this calculation only.
  • Supabase / Firebase — secure database and push notifications.
  • Sentry — crash/error reports for app stability.

5. Data retention

  • Account data: kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data — profile, driver documents and document photos, notifications, support tickets and login details — is deleted immediately and permanently. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
  • Trip and payment records (pickup/drop, bid, timestamps, payment amounts): after account deletion these are kept only in anonymised form — your name, phone number and email are removed from them — for as long as needed for safety, dispute records, and legal/accounting (tax) requirements, then deleted.
  • Documents are kept while your driver account is active, and are deleted immediately and permanently when you delete your account.
  • Location: we do not collect or store live location updates.

6. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, or a correction, from Help & Support in the app. You can delete your account and your data yourself at any time from Help & Support → Delete My Data in the app. Account deletion is immediate and permanent — once deleted, your account and personal data cannot be recovered.

7. Consent

By creating an account, you confirm you are 18 years or older (or have verifiable parental consent) and you agree to the collection and use of your data as described in this policy. Consent is taken at signup via the in-app confirmation and can be withdrawn at any time by deleting your account (Help & Support → Delete My Data) — withdrawal does not affect processing already done while consent was in place.
